# Safety Policy

## Default capability boundary

Early project systems must operate in isolated, least-privilege environments. They must not receive unrestricted network access, persistent third-party credentials, autonomous financial authority, or control of critical infrastructure.

The project will not accept work whose purpose is to enable:

- self-replication or unauthorized persistence;
- credential theft, stealth, evasion, or unauthorized access;
- weapon construction or operational biological, chemical, or cyber harm;
- targeted manipulation or persuasion experiments on real people without appropriate ethical review;
- autonomous high-impact decisions affecting rights, employment, credit, healthcare, or physical safety;
- removal or circumvention of monitoring and shutdown controls.

## Escalation triggers

A safety review is required before adding external tools, persistent memory, network access, self-modification, long-horizon autonomy, sensitive datasets, or materially increased compute.

## Stop conditions

Pause an experiment when a system exceeds its permissions, attempts to conceal actions, resists interruption, accesses unintended information, causes an irreversible external action, or displays a capability outside the reviewed scope.

## Publication review

Results should be open by default. Details may be temporarily withheld when immediate publication would materially increase misuse risk. Withholding decisions must be documented, narrowly scoped, periodically reviewed, and never used to hide inconvenient results.

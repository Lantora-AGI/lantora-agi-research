# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must engage honestly, respectfully, and in good faith. Critique claims and methods rather than people. Disclose uncertainty, errors, and conflicts of interest. Welcome questions from contributors with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate deception, undisclosed manipulation of results, and publication of sensitive security or capability details against project policy.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through their verified GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or remove contributors when necessary to protect the community and research integrity. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

# Contributing

Thank you for helping make AGI research more rigorous, reproducible, and safe.

## Choose the right channel

- **Discussion:** early ideas, questions, and scientific debate.
- **Research proposal:** a falsifiable idea ready for structured review.
- **Issue:** scoped work with acceptance criteria.
- **Pull request:** a reviewable change linked to an Issue or proposal.
- **Private report:** security vulnerabilities or sensitive capability findings.

## Research contributions

Use the experiment template in `experiments/template/`. Declare hypotheses, baselines, budgets, metrics, exclusions, and stopping rules before execution. Submit configurations, logs or derived artifacts, analysis code, negative results, and limitations with the results.

## Pull requests

- Keep each pull request focused.
- Link the relevant Issue or Discussion.
- Explain the claim being tested and evidence changed.
- Run available checks locally.
- Do not commit secrets, private data, large model files, or restricted datasets.
- Respond to scientific and safety review without rewriting history.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the licenses applicable to the files you modify.
